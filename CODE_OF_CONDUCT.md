# 社区行为规范 / Community standards

欢迎不同经验、背景和语言的参与者。规范适用于本项目的 Issue、PR 和其他公开协作空间。

- 围绕事实与代码讨论，批评方案而非攻击个人。
- 尊重他人的时间、署名和选择，给新人具体、可操作的帮助。
- 不骚扰、歧视、威胁、泄露私人信息或持续发布与项目无关的内容。
- 意见不一致时说明依据；维护者解释项目范围和最终取舍。

需要维护者处理时，可在相关讨论中请求介入；涉及骚扰或私人信息，可使用 GitHub 的 Report content 功能，无需公开重复敏感内容。维护者可以提醒、隐藏不当内容、锁定讨论或限制参与，并在适当情况下说明处理理由。

## English

People of different experience levels, backgrounds, and languages are welcome. These standards apply to this project's issues, PRs, and other public collaboration spaces.

- Discuss evidence and code; criticize proposals rather than people.
- Respect time, authorship, and choices. Give newcomers actionable help.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, expose private information, or repeatedly post unrelated content.
- Explain disagreements. Maintainers explain the project's scope and final decisions.

Request maintainer intervention in the relevant discussion when needed. For harassment or private information, use GitHub's Report content feature without reposting sensitive material. Maintainers may remind participants, hide inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation, explaining decisions when appropriate.
